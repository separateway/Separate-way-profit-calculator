How every number is calculated
  • Net revenue (ex-GST) = Selling price ÷ (1 + product GST rate). The GST part is collected for the government, not your income.
  • Purchase and packaging cost = cost ex-GST. If ITC is off, the input GST is added as a cost. If ITC is on, it is excluded so recoverable GST is never counted twice.
  • Referral fee = rate × selling price (GST-inclusive or ex-GST, per your setting). FBA/Easy Ship, storage and inbound fees are entered ex-GST.
  • GST on Amazon services = (referral + fulfilment + storage + inbound + ads) × fee GST rate. Cost only when ITC is off.
  • Returns and damage = allowance % × net revenue.
  • Net profit = net revenue − purchase − packaging − Amazon fees − unrecovered GST − ads − returns − other.
  • Margin = profit ÷ net revenue. ROI = profit ÷ (purchase + packaging + storage + inbound).
  • Break-even and target price: profit is a straight line of the selling price, so the tool solves it exactly (profit = 0, or profit = target).
  • Max ad spend = profit before ads ÷ (1 + unrecovered GST on ads). Break-even ACoS = max ad spend ÷ selling price.
  • Estimated vs actual: this tool only estimates. Enter the real profit from your Amazon settlement report to see the gap.
